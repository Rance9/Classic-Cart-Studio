## Escalation decision

The recheck found changes in most records of every group, including M3.2's low-risk records and records M3.3 had already audited. The causes recur: unsupported specifics from encyclopedia text, names that differ from the US manual, invented details and other-version details. Under the escalation rule, every remaining previously audited, corrected or recovered record (347) was audited in full in M3.4, so all 773 overviews received an independent audit.
