# M3.2 official description coverage

**101 of 773** default-catalog games have a verified official description: 96 from Nintendo of Europe's NES product pages (kept as accepted) and **5 new in M3.2**. The new ones come from original NES manual scans (read page by page) or the developer's own page. No text was written or edited by a model; one OCR artifact was normalized and is recorded.

**Declined candidates:**
- **BMX Simulator:** the record's release is ambiguous, and the text came from a compilation's manual.
- **Magic Carpet 1001:** the text came from a different product's manual, the Caltron 6-in-1.

**Not attempted at scale:** most of the 106 manuals used in M3.2 have usable story text, but their OCR is too noisy to quote verbatim without reading each scan by hand. The milestone ranks official expansion third, after accuracy and overview coverage. Candidates could be reviewed in a later pass.

**Known regional conflict:**
- **Adventure Island:** the official Nintendo of Europe text names the princess Tina.
- The US manual, which matches the catalog's USA record, names her Leilani, and the Game overview follows the US manual.
- Both texts are accurate for their region and now appear together. This needs an owner decision.
