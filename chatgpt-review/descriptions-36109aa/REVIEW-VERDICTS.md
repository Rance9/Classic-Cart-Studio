# Review verdicts for the 53-game sample (private)

**Reviewer:** the agent, reading each text as displayed against the catalog identity and the private research material (official page text, encyclopedia sections). These are not owner verdicts. **The owner's human review of this sample is still open** (see the report, "Records requiring manual review").

**What was checked for each game:**
- right game and NES version
- every concrete statement traceable to the sources
- neutral wording (no praise, reception or marketing)
- US spelling, grammar and readability
- missing-text messages correct

**Verdicts:**
- **Accept**: no change needed.
- **Accept, note**: correct, with a remark for the owner.
- **Corrected**: a grammar-only correction, applied at assembly and recorded in the private dataset.
- **Exception confirmed**: no text is shown, and that is correct.
